# Adding CovaMac Amazon Affiliate Gear to Framer

This guide shows you how to add your **curated Amazon affiliate accessories** to your Framer landing page website in less than 2 minutes.

---

## Method 1: Instant HTML Embed (Recommended & Easiest)

Framer allows you to paste HTML/CSS/JS directly onto your canvas using the **Embed** component.

### Step 1: Open Your Framer Project
1. Open your CovaMac project in Framer (you currently have it open in Google Chrome).
2. Scroll to where you want the gear section to appear (e.g., right below your download button or before the footer).

### Step 2: Insert the Embed Component
1. In the top toolbar, click **Insert** (or press `I` on your keyboard).
2. Search for **Embed** (under Utility).
3. Drag the **Embed** component onto your page where you want the section.
4. In the right-hand panel, set:
   - **Type**: `HTML`
   - **Width**: `Fill (100%)`
   - **Height**: `Auto` (or `600px`)

### Step 3: Paste the Code
1. Open [`framer/framer_embed_widget.html`](framer_embed_widget.html).
2. Copy the entire file contents (`Cmd + A`, `Cmd + C`).
3. In Framer's Embed code editor, paste the code (`Cmd + V`).
4. Click outside or press Save.

The widget will render with:
- Glassmorphic dark Apple theme matching CovaMac.
- Automatic 8-second rotation across all 13 Amazon affiliate products.
- Interactive `◀` and `▶` buttons so visitors can browse gear.
- Official Amazon orange "View on Amazon ↗" buttons opening your affiliate tracking links (`amzn.to`).
- Amazon Operating Agreement disclosure notice at the bottom.
